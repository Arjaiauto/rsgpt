Patterns, not proof.Guest fame, news cycles and the YouTube algorithm move views too. Use these to ask better questions, not as rules.

Every episode gets a fair score: its views divided by the typical views of the channel's episodes at the same age. 2.0× means twice a typical episode of that age.
