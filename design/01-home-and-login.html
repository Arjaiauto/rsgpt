How it was built

From 500 episodes to one search box

Every episode went through the same six steps. Each step is checked by code before the next one starts, so nothing reaches the search box that can't be traced back to a moment in an episode.

  1. 1

    Episodes

    499

    of the latest 500 episodes transcribed; one age-restricted episode skipped.

  2. 2

    Transcripts

    1.04M

    timed lines, from YouTube captions first and audio transcription where captions were missing.

  3. 3

    Hindi

    676,500

    Hindi lines written in Roman Hinglish and translated to English, so any phrasing finds them.

  4. 4

    Speakers

    99%

    of lines labelled host or guest, so opinions are credited to the right person.

  5. 5

    Extraction

    36,797

    takeaways, quotes, clips and questions pulled out; 98.3% passed the timestamp and exact-quote checks.

  6. 6

    Search

    35,234

    searchable passages in English, Hinglish and Hindi; most answers arrive in 1 to 3 seconds.
